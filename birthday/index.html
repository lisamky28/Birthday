A Letter for You 💌

To my favourite human,

Happy birthday, my love. ❤️

I wish you could borrow my eyes for a little while, just so you could see how lovely you are to me. Not only on your best days, not only when you're smiling, but on the ordinary days too — when you're tired, quiet, distracted, or simply being yourself.

You have this way of making little things feel important. A song becomes a memory. A random conversation becomes something I replay with a smile. An ordinary evening starts feeling like a scene I want to keep forever.

If our story were a movie, I wouldn't ask for a perfect script. I'd want the unexpected turns, the silly arguments over music, the missed turns that lead to beautiful places, the laughter that makes our stomachs hurt, and the quiet moments when being beside you is enough.

I hope this year brings you courage for the dreams you haven't told everyone about, peace on the days that feel heavy, and so many reasons to be proud of the person you're becoming.

And if I could make one wish for myself, it would be for more ordinary days with you — because somehow, with you, ordinary never feels ordinary.

Always a little more yours, ♡

Scene One — The Long Drive

No strict plans. No checking the time. Just taking the scenic route because the sunset is pretty and neither of us wants the moment to end.

Scene Two — The Tiny Things

Sharing snacks, stealing glances, singing the wrong lyrics with full confidence, and laughing so much that we forget what was funny.

Scene Three — The Quiet

The song gets softer. The sky gets darker. Your hand finds mine, and for once, neither of us needs to fill the silence.

My Favourite Ending

Not a grand movie ending. Just us reaching home, still talking in the driveway, saying “five more minutes” because goodbye can wait a little.
